# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Anole community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks, trolling, public or private intimidation, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
