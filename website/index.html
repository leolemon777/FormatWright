Local conversion, with receipts. 本地转换，结果有据可查。

Anole converts your files entirely on your machine — and then proves it. Every job ends with a machine-readable acceptance report: text layer extractable, fonts embedded, page counts conserved, archive entries accounted for. No cloud. No telemetry. No “trust me.” Anole 完全在本机完成转换，并且用证据说话：每个任务结束时都会生成机器可读的验收报告——文字层可提取、字体已嵌入、页数守恒、归档条目一件不少。没有云端，没有遥测，也没有「相信我」。

§ 01 · Why Anole§ 01 · 为什么是 Anole

Three things other converters don’t do别的转换器不做的那三件事

  • 01

    Receipts, not exit codes要回执，不要退出码

    A zero exit code is not proof of success. After every conversion, Anole independently validates the output and writes a machine-readable acceptance report you can archive, diff, and audit. 退出码为零不等于转换成功。每次转换之后，Anole 都会独立校验输出，并写下可归档、可比对、可审计的机器可读验收报告。

    Evidence证据 Merging two PDFs? Page-count conservation is re-measured by an independent Poppler pass — 7 pages in, 7 pages out, or the job fails. 合并两份 PDF？页数守恒由独立的 Poppler 复测：进去 7 页、出来 7 页，差一页即判失败。

  • 02

    Local by default, quiet by design默认本地，天生安静

    Your files never leave the machine. Conversion engines run as network-isolated subprocesses, and the app keeps working with the network forcibly offline — no telemetry, no analytics, no phoning home. 文件绝不离开你的电脑。转换引擎以网络隔离的子进程运行，强制断网也照常工作——没有遥测、没有统计上报、没有任何回传。

    Evidence证据 Zero network requests of its own: the desktop app and the CLI make no calls anywhere. 自身零网络请求：桌面端与 CLI 不向任何地方发起调用。

  • 03

    An honest engine supply chain引擎供应链，诚实为本

    Anole deliberately ships without bundled third-party engines — their licenses and supply chains stay in the open, not hidden inside an installer. It discovers engines already on your system, or activates an auditable starter pack. Anole 刻意不捆绑第三方引擎——引擎的许可证与供应链摊在明处，而不是藏进安装包。它在你自己的系统上发现引擎，或激活可审查的 starter pack。

    Evidence证据 Every engine pack pins hashes and licenses, ships a deterministic SPDX 2.3 SBOM plus a sources.json provenance sidecar, and is re-verified at every start. 每个引擎包都固定哈希与许可证，随附确定性 SPDX 2.3 SBOM 和 sources.json 来源清单，且每次启动重新校验。

§ 03 · Engines§ 03 · 引擎

Discovered on your system, not bundled in the box在系统上发现，而不是装进盒子里

Third-party binaries carry their own license and supply-chain obligations — GPL, LGPL and MPL components, and Microsoft Edge may not be redistributed at all. So Anole ships without engines on purpose and finds them on your host instead. Discovered engines are reported as unverified by design, and the doctor never downloads anything. 第三方二进制自带许可证与供应链义务——GPL、LGPL、MPL 组件，而 Microsoft Edge 根本不允许再分发。所以 Anole 有意不带引擎发行，转而在你的主机上发现它们。被发现的引擎按设计标记为 unverified，doctor 永不下载任何东西。
